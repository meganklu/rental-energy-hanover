Send one email covering all of these rather than one per item. Ask your landlord has what to put in it.

    Fit adhesive-backed weatherstripping around the frame of the exterior door and a door sweep along the bottom of it. The materials are under $25 and I would buy them and fit them myself. It mostly comes off again at move-out.
