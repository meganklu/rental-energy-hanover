Renter basics

  • Read your bill

    kWh, rate, and what's actually driving your electric bill.

  • Understanding your heating system

    What kind of heat you have, how to tell by looking, and what it means for your bill.

  • Find your drafts

    You can't seal a gap you haven't found.

  • Seal your windows with film

    A plastic film kit over a drafty window, on in an hour, off at move-out.

    No permission needed Comes off at move-out
    Under $25 1 to 2 hours Medium impact

  • Seal your door with a sweep

    Weatherstripping and a door sweep close the gap most doors leak through.

    Ask your landlord first Mostly comes off at move-out
    Under $25 1 to 2 hours Medium impact

  • Set your thermostat back

    Turning the heat down while you are out or asleep typically uses less energy, free.

    No permission needed Comes off at move-out
    Free Under 30 minutes High impact

  • Keep your radiator or baseboard clear

    Furniture and curtains in front of a heat source keep the heat from reaching the room.

    No permission needed Comes off at move-out
    Free Under 30 minutes Medium impact

  • Use a space heater safely

    Costs more to run than central heat, and needs real clearance. Safety first.

    No permission needed Comes off at move-out
    $25 to $75 Under 30 minutes Medium impact

  • Cut your hot water use

    Shower length, laundry temperature, and a dripping tap all affect the bill.

    No permission needed Comes off at move-out
    Free Under 30 minutes Medium impact

  • Cut phantom load

    A power strip you can switch off stops plugged-in devices from drawing power all day.

    No permission needed Comes off at move-out
    Under $25 Under 30 minutes Low impact

  • Swap in LED bulbs

    LEDs use a fraction of the electricity incandescent bulbs do. Keep the originals in a box.

    No permission needed Comes off at move-out
    Under $25 Under 30 minutes Low impact
