Cut phantom load

A power strip you can switch off stops plugged-in devices from drawing power all day.

No permission needed Comes off at move-out
Under $25 Under 30 minutes Low impact

What you need

  • A power strip with its own on/off switch

Where to get it: home and hardware stores, or ordered online for delivery (Home Depot, Lowe's, Ace, Amazon, Walmart and Target all deliver to Hanover). Home Depot in West Lebanon is reachable on the fare-free Advance Transit bus.

Steps

  1. Group chargers, a game console, a TV, or a monitor onto one switched power strip.
  2. Flip the strip off when you leave for the day or head to bed.
  3. Leave anything that needs to stay on, like a fridge, on its own separate outlet.

Why this works

Many devices draw a small amount of power even while off or in standby, just to keep a clock, a light, or a remote sensor ready. Plugged-in chargers with nothing attached still pull a little too. A single device is barely noticeable, but a room full of them plugged in around the clock adds up quietly on a bill you are already paying. A switched power strip lets you cut all of it at once instead of unplugging each thing by hand.
