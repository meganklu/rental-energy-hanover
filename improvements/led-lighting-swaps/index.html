Swap in LED bulbs

LEDs use a fraction of the electricity incandescent bulbs do. Keep the originals in a box.

No permission needed Comes off at move-out
Under $25 Under 30 minutes Low impact

What you need

  • LED bulbs matching the base and brightness of your existing bulbs
  • A small box to keep the originals in

Where to get it: ordered online for delivery, or in person at the West Lebanon stores, reachable on the fare-free Advance Transit bus. Where to get it has the addresses and the routes.

Steps

  1. Check the base type and wattage printed on your current bulbs before you shop.
  2. Unscrew each incandescent bulb and set it aside.
  3. Screw in the LED replacement.
  4. Put every original bulb in a labeled box so you can swap back at move-out.

Why this works

An incandescent bulb makes light by heating a filament, which wastes most of its energy as heat rather than light. An LED produces the same brightness from a fraction of the electricity, and lasts far longer, so the swap keeps paying off every time the light is on.

Savings

Estimates are typical ranges, not guarantees. What you actually save depends on your building, your heating system, and the weather.

The U.S. Department of Energy states that LED bulbs use up to 80% less energy than incandescent bulbs.
