Keep your radiator or baseboard clear

Furniture and curtains in front of a heat source keep the heat from reaching the room.

No permission needed Comes off at move-out
Free Under 30 minutes Medium impact

What you need

  • Nothing to buy. Just some rearranging.

Steps

  1. Walk the unit and check every radiator or baseboard heater for anything in front of it.
  2. Move couches, desks, and other furniture at least a few inches clear.
  3. Pull long curtains back so they do not hang over a baseboard unit.
  4. Keep the area clear through the season, not just once.
Why this works

A radiator or baseboard heater warms a room by letting heat rise and circulate off its surface. Furniture or curtains against it trap that heat right where it is made, so the heater works harder while the rest of the room stays cold, and the thermostat keeps calling for more heat than the room actually needs.

Savings: Estimates are typical ranges, not guarantees. What you actually save depends on your building, your heating system, and the weather.
