# Content Strategy

> **Status:** ● Complete · **Last updated:** 2026-08-19 · **Owner:** Megan

## 1. Content principles

1. **Every recommendation states whether it needs landlord permission.** Recommendations are organized into categories based on how much effort they require and whether only the landlord can implement them.
2. **Every recommendation states cost and effort in student terms.** For example, "$12, 20 minutes, reversible." Information about whether the improvement can be done independently or requires a contractor/additional help is also included.
3. **Nothing permanent, nothing that risks the deposit** unless clearly flagged as such. Upgrades that renters can advocate for and request from their landlord are included with clear instructions on how to go about this.
4. **Local specifics over general advice.** Recommendations are based on local utilities and weather.
5. **We cite; we don't guess.** Citations and links are provided for more information.

## 2. Voice and tone

| Attribute | We are | We are not |
|---|---|---|
| Direct | directly state benefits, provide easily implementable steps toward upgrades | overwhelming with information and specifics on the main pages |
| Practical | recommendations organized and scaled appropriately for student renters | assumptions of resources and time for significant upgrades |
| Non-preachy | talks about money and comfort first | guilt-driven climate messaging |
| Educational | provides useful context and information for each recommendation | assumption of prior knowledge about homeownership and property maintenance |

**Reading level target:** Plain language, ~grade 8, short sentences and bullet points, use of figures and illustrations

**Rewrite example** — One paragraph of existing weatherization guidance rewritten in
this project's voice.

Weatherizing your home involves physical home improvements—such as insulating and air sealing most often in basement and attic areas—to resolve issues like ice dams, moisture, mold, cold spots and drafts. It prevents indoor air from escaping and outdoor air from entering your home.

> **After:** What is Weatherizing?
The purpose of weatherizing is to prevent indoor air from escaping and outdoor air from entering your home, which helps save on energy costs. Weatherizing your home involves physical home improvements, such as adding insulation and air sealing cracks, especially in basement and attic areas. These changes resolve issues like ice dams, moisture, mold, cold spots and drafts.

## 4. Sourcing and accuracy policy

**Acceptable sources, in order of preference:**
1. Sustainable Hanover and Town of Hanover published material
2. NH state agencies and NH utility program pages
3. U.S. DOE / Energy Star / EIA
4. Other state agencies and energy initiatives
5. Peer-reviewed and national-lab research

**Not acceptable:**
Vendor blogs, product marketing, undated listicles, AI-generated summaries

**Rules:**
- Every numeric claim (cost, savings, temperature, R-value) has a citation in [sources.md](sources.md).
- Savings figures are stated as **ranges with their assumptions visible** ("typically 5–15%, in a
  unit with single-pane windows") instead of a single number or a promise.
- Anything about NH tenant law, utility rates, or rebate programs carries a **last-verified date**
  and is re-checked per the review cadence in [sources.md](sources.md).
- Local-specific claims (weather, fuel mix, utility) are checked against a NH/Upper Valley source,
  not a national average.
- **Out-of-state programs are never presented as available here.** Material from other states
  (category 4 above) may be used for framing, analogy, or evidence that an approach works —
  but a program a Hanover renter cannot enroll in must be labeled as another state's program,
  and paired with the NH equivalent if one exists.

## 5. Legal and safety posture

- **Legal advice:** We describe general NH tenant protections and link to authoritative sources.
  We never advise on a specific dispute, never interpret a particular lease, and never suggest
  a course of action against a named landlord. Any page mentioning rights carries the legal
  disclaimer below and a link to a real help resource.
- **Safety-critical topics** (space heaters, carbon monoxide, blocking vents or baseboards,
  electrical load, covering radiators, plastic film near heat sources): safety framing appears
  *before* the instructions, not in a footnote. Requires the sign-off in §4.
- **We never recommend anything that defeats a safety device**: no blocking vents, no covering
  CO or smoke detectors, no sealing a room that has a combustion appliance in it.

**Standing disclaimers** — use this exact wording site-wide:

> **Legal:** This site provides general information, not legal advice. Leases and situations
> differ.

> **Savings:** Estimates are typical ranges, not guarantees. What you actually save depends on
> your building, your heating system, and the weather.

> **Permission:** Check your lease before making any change. When in doubt, ask your landlord in
> writing.

**Where disclaimers appear:**

| Disclaimer | Appears on |
|---|---|
| Legal | Any page mentioning rights, habitability, deposits, or leases |
| Savings | Any page showing a number, and in any calculator output |
| Permission | Every "Improvement" page where `landlordPermission` is `ask` or `yes` |

- **Do we ever name specific landlords or properties?** No. See non-goals in
  [project-brief.md](project-brief.md).
- **Product recommendations:** Generic categories only ("V-seal weatherstripping", "window
  insulation film kit") — no brand endorsements and no affiliate links. Naming a retailer as
  a place to find a category is fine; naming a product as the one to buy is not.

## 6. Content model

### Type: "Improvement" (the core unit)

| Field | Key | Required | Type / allowed values |
|---|---|---|---|
| Title | `title` | ✓ | Verb-first, under 50 chars — "Seal your windows with film" |
| Summary | `summary` | ✓ | One sentence, under 140 chars. Says what it does and roughly what it costs |
| Cost | `cost` | ✓ | `free` \| `under25` \| `25to75` \| `over75` |
| Time | `time` | ✓ | `under30min` \| `1to2hr` \| `afternoon` \| `contractor` |
| Difficulty | `difficulty` | ✓ | `easy` \| `moderate` \| `needs-help` |
| Landlord permission | `landlordPermission` | ✓ | `none` \| `ask` \| `required` |
| Reversible at move-out | `reversible` | ✓ | `fully` \| `mostly` \| `no` |
| Estimated impact | `impact` | ✓ | `low` \| `medium` \| `high` \| `enabler` — plus optional `impactNote` for a cited range |
| Heating type | `appliesToHeat` | ✓ | array: `electric-baseboard` \| `forced-air` \| `radiator` \| `heat-pump` \| `any` |
| Who pays | `appliesToPayer` | ✓ | `tenant` \| `included` \| `any` — filters out advice that saves the landlord money, not the student |
| Lifecycle phase | `phase` | ✓ | `hunting` \| `signing` \| `move-in` \| `pre-winter` \| `deep-winter` \| `break` \| `spring` \| `move-out` |
| Steps | `steps` | ✓ | Ordered list, imperative, one action per step |
| Materials | `materials` | | Generic categories only (see §5) |
| Safety notes | `safety` | | Rendered *above* steps, never below |
| Sources | `sources` | ✓ | array of IDs from [sources.md](sources.md) |
| Last reviewed | `lastReviewed` | ✓ | ISO date |

**Validation rule:** an Improvement with an empty `sources` array, or with a number in
`impactNote` and no `sources`, fails `tools/check-content.mjs` and does not get merged. The site
has no build step, so this check is the gate. See [architecture.md](architecture.md) §4 for how
the fields are stored and validated.

**`appliesToPayer` is the field that earns this project its keep.** If utilities are included in
rent, a shorter shower saves the student nothing — and telling them otherwise is how the site
loses credibility on first contact.

### Other content types

| Type | Purpose | Distinguishing fields |
|---|---|---|
| `Explainer` | Concept pages — how a bill works, what setback is | `question` (the plain-language question it answers), `misconception` (optional) |
| `Program` | NHSaves, income-eligible weatherization, assistance | `eligibility`, `whoRuns`, `state`, `renterEligible`, `landlordApprovalNeeded`, `applyUrl`, `lastReviewed` |
| `ChecklistItem` | Steps in a generated checklist | `phase`, `improvementRef`, `roommateVisible` |

| `Question` | Situation selector inputs | `prompt`, `options`, `mapsToField` |

**`Program.state` exists specifically so out-of-state programs cannot be rendered as local.**
Anything with `state !== "NH"` renders with an explicit label
or doesn't render at all.

## 7. Media

- **Photos:** Prefer original photos of real Hanover-area rentals.
- **Illustrations/diagrams:** SVG images generated for this site. Use animation and dynamic visuals when possible.
- **Alt text policy:** Every image has alt text describing what matters energy-wise and the
  scene. "Gap under a door with daylight visible", not "a photo of a door." Decorative images
  get `alt=""`. See [accessibility.md](accessibility.md) §2.
- **Video:** Out of scope for v1.

### Where students actually get materials

**Online.** Home Depot, Lowe's, Ace, Amazon, Walmart and Target all deliver to Hanover. This
will be the easiest option for most students. Per §5 we name retailers as places to find a
category of product, and we use no affiliate links.

**In person, by fare-free bus.** Advance Transit runs to West Lebanon at no cost, which puts
Home Depot and LaValley Building Supply within reach without a car. Pair this with the
pre-winter checklist (topic #8) so students can make one trip in October with a list.

Suggest ordering or shopping by mid-October so materials arrive before the cold, using the
heating season dates above.

**Content implication:** topics #2, #3, #12, #15 and #17 all involve a purchase. Each needs a
"where to get it" block. A single shared materials-sourcing page (topic #19) keeps this in one
place, so the improvement pages can link to it instead of repeating it.
