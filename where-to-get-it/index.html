Where to get it

Nothing on this site needs a car, but a few things need planning. Here is where the materials actually are.

Order it online

Everything this site recommends is a stocked category rather than a specialty item. Window insulation film kits, V-seal weatherstripping, door sweeps, LED bulbs and switched power strips all deliver to Hanover from the usual home and general retailers. Order a few days before you need them, because the week the first cold snap arrives is the week the film kits sell out.

Search the generic name rather than a brand. "Window insulation film kit" and "V-seal weatherstripping" are what the products are called, and this site does not recommend a specific one.

Go in person in West Lebanon

The nearest full selection is on the Route 12A strip and around it in West Lebanon.

  • The Home Depot, 296 Plainfield Road (Route 12A), West Lebanon. The widest stock of weatherstripping, film, sweeps and bulbs in the area.
  • LaValley Building Supply, 5 Airport Road, West Lebanon. A building supply yard rather than a big-box store, and closer to the Kilton Library end of West Lebanon.

Getting there without a car

Advance Transit runs fare-free across the Upper Valley. There is no ticket to buy and no pass to show. The Blue route connects Hanover and Lebanon on weekdays at roughly fifteen-minute frequency, and West Lebanon's hub is at the Kilton Library.

Check Advance Transit's own route map for which service reaches the Route 12A stores and how far you would be walking from the nearest stop. Service is weekday-only on most routes, so a Saturday trip needs a different plan.

Plan the return trip before you go. Carrying a window film kit and a door sweep is easy. Waiting an hour for the next bus with them is less so.

What each improvement needs

  • Seal your windows with film: a film kit sized to your window, scissors, a hair dryer.
  • Seal your door with a sweep: weatherstripping and a door sweep.
  • Swap in LED bulbs: LED bulbs in the right base and shape. Keep the originals in a box for move-out.
  • Cut phantom load: a power strip with a switch.
  • Set your thermostat back and keep your heat source clear: nothing at all.

Permission: Check your lease before making any change. When in doubt, ask your landlord in writing. Anything that screws or sticks to a door frame is worth asking about before you buy it.
