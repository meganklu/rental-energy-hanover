Read your bill

Renter basics

Two numbers on your electric bill explain almost everything: how much you used, and what each unit of that costs. Multiply them and you have your bill.

Where to find each number

  • Usage in kWh, usually near the middle of the bill, sometimes with a chart of the last several months.
  • The rate, in cents or dollars per kWh, often printed near the usage figure or in a rate schedule.
  • The total, at the bottom, which is usage times rate plus any fixed monthly charges.

True or false: the total dollar amount on your bill tells you how much electricity you used.

False. The dollar total mixes your usage with the rate and any fixed charges. The kWh figure is the actual amount of energy you used, and it is the number that changes when you change a habit.
